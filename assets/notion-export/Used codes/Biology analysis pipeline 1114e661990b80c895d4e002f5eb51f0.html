Biology analysis pipeline

Calculate FRiP scores for DAP-seq or ChIP-seq data

With single-base methylation level file, to identify methylation regions that have certain proportion of cytosines methylated higher than a threshold in the region.

In single-cell analysis, to make pseudobulk bigwig

  1. to split merged bam files into single-cell bams according to the CB tags
  1. to gather the single-cell bam files into the same folder if they are the same celltype, based on the meta information to create softlink in new folders
  1. to merge the bam files together for each cell type

Length distribution of fastq reads

For scaleRNA-seq, to extract barcode information from R1 reads, and correct them according to whitelist if the mismatch falls bellow the threshold. Detect ligation, pcr and RT barcode and UMI at the same time.

RT barcode: 8nt away from linker sequence

ligation: first 9nt of R1 reads

PCR: the whole length of I1 reads

UMIs: roughly the 8nt sequences after linker (can be corrected afterwards)
